How it works

One app for people who need cleaning and people who clean

Clients book cleanings. Cleaners choose the jobs they want. Clean Connect handles scheduling, messaging, secure payment and reviews.

For clients

  1. Tell us about your spaceA quick quiz covers your home size, the type of cleaning, how often and your budget.
  2. Pick a cleaner, day and timeBrowse profiles with ratings, reviews and hourly rates, or post your request to cleaners nearby.
  3. Your cleaner arrivesMessage them in the app and get notified when they check in.
  4. Pay after it’s doneYour card is charged only after the cleaning is complete. Rate and tip in the app.

For cleaners

  1. Set up your profileAdd your services, hourly rate, availability and travel distance.
  2. Verify your identityUpload a photo ID and selfie and consent to a background check.
  3. Choose your jobsSee the pay, hours and location before you accept. No bidding.
  4. Get paidEarnings are sent to your bank account or debit card after each completed job.

Policies

Cancellations, refunds and disputes

Cancellation policy

  • Clients may cancel for free up to 24 hours before the scheduled start time.
  • If a confirmed cleaning is cancelled less than 24 hours before the start time, a $15 late-cancellation fee is charged. The full fee goes to the cleaner for their lost time.
  • Requests that no cleaner has accepted yet can be cancelled at any time for free.
  • If a cleaner cancels, the client is never charged and we help find another cleaner.
  • Recurring cleanings can be paused or stopped at any time in the app.

Refund policy

  • Clean Connect Guarantee: if you’re not satisfied, report it in the app or by email within 24 hours of the cleaning. We’ll arrange a free re-clean of the missed areas.
  • If a re-clean isn’t possible or doesn’t resolve the issue, we’ll issue a partial or full refund based on what was reported.
  • If a cleaner doesn’t show up, you are not charged.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original card, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
  • Tips can be refunded only if the cleaning was not completed.

Dispute process

  • Email us at the address below with your booking date and what happened. Photos help.
  • We reply within 1 business day and aim to resolve every dispute within 5 business days.
  • We review messages, check-in and check-out times and photos from both the client and the cleaner before deciding.
  • For property damage, report it within 48 hours so we can review it with the cleaner.

Promotions

  • Code CLEAN10 gives $10 off a client’s first booking.
  • One use per customer and household. It can’t be exchanged for cash.
  • Recurring discounts apply automatically and can be combined with CLEAN10 on a first booking.
  • Clean Connect may change or end promotions at any time. Bookings already confirmed keep their price.
